Key Assurances
Officers remain in control

No transaction is approved or rejected without a human having the ability to intervene. The AI recommends — the officer decides.

When in doubt, escalate

If the AI is not confident in its assessment, it automatically escalates to human review rather than guessing. Accuracy is always prioritised over speed.

Built for regulators

Designed around FATF Recommendations 6 and 10, and Trade-Based Money Laundering guidance — the international standards your regulators audit against.

Privacy by design

Sensitive financial data — company names, amounts, account details — is never written to logs. Every system access is auditable.

This is a decision-support tool

Legal accountability stays with the compliance officer. The AI assists; it does not replace professional judgement.
